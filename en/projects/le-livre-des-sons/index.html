Le Livre des Sons

Book · 2026

Le Livre des Sons proposes a system of explicit, verifiable rules of correspondence between visual and sonic parameters. It leads to a piece of software, SIGN.

Over 400 pages in three parts: an experimental laboratory of 23 studies and 66 plates, an essay on perceptual regimes, and openings. [TO CONFIRM: figures and part titles]

The book is extended by a website, reached by QR code, where the plates can be heard.
