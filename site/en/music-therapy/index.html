Music therapy

I am a clinical music therapist with a psychoanalytic orientation. My work draws in particular on Winnicott, Bion, Anzieu and Stern.

What I offer

  • Music-making workshops in institutions, through to recording and releasing the pieces.
  • Sessions of listening, handling and exploring sound, focused on well-being.
  • Sound hypnosis sessions.
  • Improvisation, pulse and rhythm games on digital instruments, in groups or one to one.
  • Sound and video projects with pupils, such as creating avatars.
